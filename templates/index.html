How DevaBhoomi Works

🏛️
01

Temple Logs Waste

Temple trustee taps 2 buttons. DevaBhoomi logs today's flower waste quantity.

🤖
02

ML Predicts & Matches

Random Forest model predicts waste for next 7 days. KNN engine finds nearest farmers instantly.

📱
03

Farmer Gets WhatsApp Alert

Matched farmers receive instant Tamil WhatsApp message with pickup details and quantity.

🌿
04

Waste Becomes Compost

Farmer collects, composts on-farm. Chemical fertilizer replaced. Zero cost. Zero waste.
